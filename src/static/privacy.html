Privacy Policy

App: Sodara Bhashalu (Kannada and Tamil for Telugu speakers)
Last updated: 24 October 2026

What the app stores

The app saves your learning progress on your own device only: module status, practice marks, notes, your study streak and display settings. This data never leaves your device unless you copy a backup code yourself.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It makes no network requests except loading its own files when used as a website.

Deleting your data

Open My Progress → Reset all progress, or uninstall the app, or clear the website’s data in your browser.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
