# Google Play listing: Kannada Learning

**App name (≤30):** Kannada Learning

**Short description (≤80):**
Learn Kannada and Tamil through Telugu, with side-by-side grammar.

**Full description (≤4000):**
Already speak Telugu? Kannada and Tamil are its sister languages, and much of what you know carries over. Kannada Learning teaches both through Telugu, side by side.

• 18 grammar modules, from the alphabet and vowel signs to tenses, cases, participles, numbers, questions and spoken forms. The sequence follows the "Learn Basics" modules of Kannada Kalike (Kannada Language Chair, JNU).
• Every example shows Telugu, Kannada and Tamil together, in native script, with a Telugu-script reading so you can pronounce every word.
• One guided path: each page has a single Back and Next, and a Learning Path map lets you jump to any step.
• Practice mode: hide the answers, then mark "Knew it" or "Missed" to build a review list.
• Track your progress separately for Kannada and Tamil, with a study streak, accuracy, a 12-week calendar and notes for each module.
• Script Converter: turn Kannada or Tamil text into Telugu script.
• Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking: your progress stays on your phone, and you can back it up with a code.
